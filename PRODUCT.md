# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and QA engineers who ship code faster than they can verify it. They're in their IDE, terminal, or CI pipeline, glancing at test results between deploys. They want speed and clarity, not hand-holding. They're technically fluent, comfortable reading stack traces and Playwright code, and they trust tools that respect their time.

## Product Purpose

MSITest is an autonomous AI testing platform. Users provide app URLs, PRDs, or natural language descriptions. The AI generates Playwright tests, executes them with full telemetry (screenshots, video, console logs, traces), and surfaces failures with root cause analysis and suggested fixes. A flakiness heatmap tracks test stability over time. Success = developers trust the test results and fix failures faster than they could with hand-written tests.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, authoritative. Clean data density, monospace for technical content, restrained color with a single blue accent, generous radii. No decoration. Information is the design.

## Anti-references

- **Not AI-ish.** No sparkle icons, magic wand metaphors, gradient text, chatbot bubbles, purple/violet accents, "powered by AI" badges as decoration, or any visual language that screams "AI tool." The AI is infrastructure, not identity. When AI analysis appears, it reads like a senior engineer's comment, not a chatbot reply.
- **Not playful.** No illustrations, whimsical empty states, or personality-forward copy. Linear, not Notion.
- **Not enterprise-gray.** Not Jenkins, not ancient CI dashboards. The blue accent and generous radii keep it modern.

## Design Principles

1. **Information is the design.** Every pixel either shows data or helps navigate to data. Decoration is waste.
2. **Technical content deserves technical typography.** Monospace for all code, test names, IDs, durations, and status. It's not a stylistic choice, it's a functional one.
3. **AI is infrastructure, not identity.** The AI works behind the scenes. Results surface as normal UI elements. When AI analysis appears, it reads like a senior engineer's comment, not a chatbot reply.
4. **Density with breath.** Tables, stats, and filters can be dense, but spacing between sections stays generous. The prototypes use 24px and 32px as section separators.
5. **Status is always visible.** Pass/fail/flaky/running states are color-coded with pills and dots. Never make the user click to learn a test's outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Good contrast ratios throughout. Motion limited to state transitions (150-200ms). No animations requiring `prefers-reduced-motion` exemptions. Semantic HTML structure. Keyboard-navigable sidebar, tables, and forms.
